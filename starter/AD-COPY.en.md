The headline and call to action appear on the banner. The body is the accompanying post. Each concept works with a scene alone or with an optional character from the same game.

## Survivalist: Invasion · Halloween

### Who left the tower light on?

One supply run before dark. Do you head for the old watchtower or turn back to shore up your base?

Button — **Play Survivalist**. Scout with a lantern.

### What’s in your pack?

A lantern for the way home, bandages for the unexpected, and room for whatever you find. What makes the cut?

Button — **Pack your bag**. Landscape only.

## Survivalist: Invasion · New Year

The storm can wait. Inside, there’s time to mend your gear and sort through the day’s finds. Where will you build your first base?

### Home before midnight

Your camp lights are in sight. So is a warehouse you haven’t searched. Take one last detour or get your haul home safe?

## Stories: Your Choice · Halloween

### Trust the captain?

It’s masquerade night. The captain holds out a key. Her ship waits at the dock. What do you ask before stepping aboard?

Button — **Make your choice**. Pirate captain.

### Keep your mask on?

Give a stranger your real name, stay behind the mask, or slip away before dawn. How does your night in the harbor begin?

Button — **Start your story**. Landscape only.

## Stories: Your Choice · New Year

### A one-way ticket?

The train pulls out on New Year’s Eve, but your traveling companion is still on the platform. Do you step off or stay aboard?

Button — **Make your choice**. Traveling cowboy.

### Who’s with you at midnight?

Snow drifts past the train window. You have time for one question. Will you ask your companion where they’re headed or who they’re running from?

Button — **Start your story**. Landscape only.

## Promotions and giveaways

For a promotion, supply the approved offer and its dates. For a giveaway, supply the prize, entry rules and dates. The post uses the wording from the brief. The ad run can have its own dates.
